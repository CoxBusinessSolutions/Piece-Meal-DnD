Build Any Character

← By-class builder

Forget classes. Set how much experience you've earned, then spend it on any mix of pieces. At level 1 you have 100 — enough for a signature or two. The powerful ideas (Extra Attack, higher-level spells) cost real earned XP and open one level ahead of yours, so you buy a level's abilities as you earn your way to it. You can't have it all — that's the point.

Commodities

The shared basics — your body and training. Fixed prices.

Features

Signature pieces from every class, deduplicated. Level-1 picks are cheap; higher-level pieces cost their real earned XP.

Ancestry

A separate, creation-only budget. Declare the ancestries you descend from — the first is your primary (it sets size and base speed) — then buy racial pieces from just those. Any one ability can rise by at most +2 from ancestry pieces.
